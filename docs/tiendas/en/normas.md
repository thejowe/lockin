# cofounder Community Guidelines


**Version:** 1.1

**Version date:** 18 October 2026

cofounder connects people so they can work out whether they could found something together. To keep it a safe place, these guidelines apply to profiles, photos, boards, answers, links and messages. They are part of the [terms of use](https://thejowe.github.io/cofounder-web/terminos).

## Zero tolerance

cofounder has **zero tolerance for abusive content and conduct**. We remove the content, suspend or delete the account and, where the law requires it, report it to the authorities.

## What is not allowed

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, insults or discrimination on any grounds.
- Sexual content, nudity or sexual propositions.
- Any content that sexualises, exploits or endangers minors.
- Graphic violence or incitement to violence or hatred.
- Impersonating another person or creating fake profiles.
- Scams, spam, soliciting money or unsolicited product promotion.
- Sharing other people's personal data without their permission.
- Using the app as a dating app or as a job board.
- Any illegal activity.

## What cofounder is and is not

cofounder is not a dating app or a job board. It does not offer legal advice or funding and does not guarantee results. The "GitHub verified" badge indicates that the GitHub link belongs to the person who posted it; it does not verify the person.

## Minimum age

You must be 16 or older. We delete the accounts of people under that age as soon as we detect them.

## Photos

The profile photo and board photos are optional. They are automatically reviewed before being published, together with the text of each board photo, and they are not published if they do not pass that review or if it cannot be done. You may only upload photos of yourself or photos you have permission to use, and on the board do not show other people without their permission.

## Child safety

Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). Any content or conduct that endangers a minor is removed, the account is suspended and it is reported to the competent authorities. The details are in our [child safety standards](https://thejowe.github.io/cofounder-web/seguridad-infantil).

## How to block and report

From anyone's profile, in the deck or in the chat, you can **block** them (they can no longer see you or write to you) or **report** them, choosing a reason and, if you want, adding some text. In the chat you can also report a specific message. You can also write to us at cofounder.soporte@gmail.com.

## What we do with a report

- We review them within 72 hours at most.
- Those that affect minors or contain sexual content, within 24 hours at most.
- Depending on the severity, we warn, remove content, or suspend or delete the account.
- We keep reports for at most 12 months from when they are created.

If you are in immediate danger, call 112. If you are a minor and need help, in Spain you can call 116 111 (Fundación ANAR).
