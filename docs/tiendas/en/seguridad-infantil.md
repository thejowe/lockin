# cofounder Child Safety Standards


**Version:** 1.0

**Version date:** 14 October 2026

## Our position

cofounder has **zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)**, including child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any conduct that sexualises, grooms or endangers minors. This page describes the standards we apply.

## Minimum age

cofounder is for people aged **16 or older**. Age is self-declared: there is no document verification or parental controls. If we detect or are told about an account belonging to someone under 16, we delete it. cofounder is not a dating app and prohibits sexual propositions.

## What we prohibit

- Any content that sexualises minors or depicts child sexual abuse or exploitation, in profiles, photos, boards, links or messages.
- Grooming, contacting or trying to contact a minor for sexual purposes or exploitation.
- Requesting, sharing or offering material of this kind.

Breaches result in the immediate removal of the content and deletion of the account, without prior notice.

## How we prevent it

- Profile and board photos are automatically reviewed before being published. The review rejects images with sexual content or signs of sexual content involving minors and, if it cannot be done, the photo is not published.
- Chat only opens between two people after a mutual match.
- Anyone can block another person: a block prevents them from seeing and writing to each other in both directions.
- The [community guidelines](https://thejowe.github.io/cofounder-web/normas) and the [terms of use](https://thejowe.github.io/cofounder-web/terminos) expressly prohibit this content.

## How to report

- **In the app:** from anyone's profile, in the deck or in the chat, choose "Report", give the reason and, if you want, some text.
- **By email:** cofounder.soporte@gmail.com, with the subject "Child safety".

## How we respond

- Reports that affect minors or contain sexual content are reviewed within **24 hours** at most.
- We remove the content and suspend or delete the account.
- We keep the report, with the internal reference of the accounts involved, for at most 12 months from when it is created.

## Legal compliance

When we become aware of child sexual abuse or exploitation content, we report it to the competent authorities and cooperate with them in accordance with applicable law. If you detect it outside the app, you can also report it to the Policía Nacional (https://www.policia.es), to the Guardia Civil or on 112 if there is immediate danger. In Spain, if you are a minor and need help, call 116 111 (Fundación ANAR).
