# cofounder Cookie Policy

**Version:** 1.0

**Version date:** 10 November 2026

## 1. Summary

The website https://getcofounder.app **does not use cookies** or any other tracking technology: no analytics, no advertising, no pixels and no social media buttons that track you. That is why we do not show you any notice asking you to accept them.

## 2. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser to remember you between visits. There are similar technologies, such as the browser's local storage or tracking pixels. The law requires asking for your consent before using those that are not strictly necessary for the service you request.

## 3. What this website uses

- **First-party cookies:** none.
- **Third-party cookies:** none. The website is served from GitHub Pages and does not load content from other services that set cookies.
- **Analytics and advertising:** none.
- **Local storage:** the public website does not store anything in your browser.

The server that hosts the website (GitHub Pages) receives, like any server, technical connection data such as your IP address in order to deliver the page to you. We do not use it to track you or to build profiles.

## 4. The app

The cofounder app for iOS and Android does not use cookies and does not include any advertising or analytics SDK. If you open the web version of the app, your session is stored in the browser's local storage so you do not have to sign in every time. This is strictly necessary to keep the session you start, it is not used to track you and you can clear it whenever you want from your browser settings (doing so signs you out).

## 5. How to clear what your browser stores

All browsers let you view and clear the cookies and local storage of each site from their privacy settings. If you clear them, the public website keeps working the same.

## 6. Changes

If we ever add cookies or technologies that are not strictly necessary, we will update this policy beforehand and ask for your prior consent, which you can refuse or withdraw.
