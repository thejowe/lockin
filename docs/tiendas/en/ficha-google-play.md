# cofounder — Google Play listing (English)

Reviewed on 7 November 2026 against the Spanish listing. Copy only the content of each field.

## App name

cofounder

## Short description

Find a cofounder: swipe profiles, match and chat as peers.

## Full description

Want to build something but need someone to build it with? cofounder helps you find a cofounder among people at a similar point to yours.

You don't need to have built anything. You can have no idea yet, an idea you haven't started, or have tried something small. Here nobody hires anybody: the relationship is between peers, and you both contribute and decide together.

YOUR PROFILE
Tell people who you are and what you bring: name, age, location and time zone, specialties, starting point, availability and level of ambition. Answer one or two short questions so your way of thinking shows, not just what you can do. If you like, add links to GitHub, your portfolio or LinkedIn.

You can also add a photo. It is optional and is only published after passing an automatic safety review.

SWIPE
Profiles appear full screen, one by one. Swipe to show you're interested in someone or to move on to the next.

MATCH AND CHAT
When the interest is mutual, you match and a private chat opens between the two of you. To break the ice, the app suggests a few questions to start with.

VERIFIED GITHUB
You can link your GitHub account to show "GitHub verified" on your profile. It indicates that the GitHub link belongs to whoever publishes it; it does not verify the person's identity or knowledge.

SAFETY AND CONTROL
You can block or report anyone from the app. You can delete your account and your data whenever you want from Settings > Account.

WHAT IT IS NOT
cofounder is not a dating app or a job board. It offers no legal advice or funding, and does not guarantee results: it helps you meet people to build with; what you do together is up to you.

For people aged 16 or over.

## Category

Type: **App**. Category: **Social** (D07). This implies complying with Google's child safety standards (public standards, in-app reporting mechanism, designated contact: D11).

## Content rating and target audience

Target audience: 16–17 and 18+ (D06). In the IARC questionnaire, declare user interaction, user-generated content, photo sharing (profile photo) and chat. Do not declare video or voice: the video-call code is no longer in the binary (T5). Do not claim a specific rating before the official result.

## Data safety, account deletion and permissions

- Data safety answers: see `cuestionario-privacidad.md`.
- Account deletion: in the app (Settings > Account > Delete my account) and web URL https://getcofounder.app/eliminar-cuenta (mandatory and working; explains how to request it by email without the app).
- Child safety (Social category): standards URL https://getcofounder.app/seguridad-infantil; designated contact soporte@getcofounder.app.
- Permissions (final binary, T5): only `CAMERA` declared by the app, for the profile photo; the gallery uses the system picker. No `RECORD_AUDIO`, `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` or notifications: there are no sensitive permissions to justify. Confirm in the AAB manifest that no library adds others (T7).
- Google UGC policies: Terms and Guidelines accepted before publishing; reporting of profiles and messages; blocking; basic text filter and photo moderation; review of reports (24 h / 72 h).
- In-app purchases: **"Contains in-app purchases: yes"** (cofounder Premium subscription, monthly or annual, with a free trial: 7 days on the annual plan, 3 on the monthly; monthly €4.99, annual €39.99; product ids `app.cofounder.mobile.premium.monthly` and `.annual`). **"Contains ads: no".** App price: free.
