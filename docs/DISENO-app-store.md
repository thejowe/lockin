# cofounder on the App Store

The first three frames show a real swipe in progress, the complementary skills in two founders’ profiles, and the chat in which they agree to test a prototype. The fourth shows how a founder can describe their own skills and availability. All public copy and on-screen content are in English.

## Art direction

Use the supplied white-on-black cofounder wordmark as a single, untinted image in each header. Keep the original app icon. On the cover, show the Discover deck during a rightward drag so the tilted card, the next profile and the Like heart are visible together. Enlarge the complementary skills on the second frame and the two chat replies on the third. The sketchbook belongs with the prototype conversation. The poster backgrounds alternate persimmon (#FF794D), warm paper (#FFF4DF), aubergine (#291A36) and lilac (#D7C7FF). Instrument Sans carries the direct part of a headline and Instrument Serif Italic gives the final phrase a human voice. Check the three headlines together at search size. [Visual comparison](../appleimatges/comparison.html)

## Delivery

Export opaque portrait PNGs at 1320 × 2868 and 1179 × 2556 to match the two iPhone display sets in the 1.0.1 draft. Follow Discover, Matches, chat and Profile with a profile answer, Groups and the mutual-match moment. Check the whole set at full size and the first three frames at search thumbnail size. Record the source of the UI images and distinguish an App Store Connect draft from an App Store release.
