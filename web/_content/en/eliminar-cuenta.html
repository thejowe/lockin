Delete your cofounder account

You can delete your account and your data from the app, or by asking us by email, without needing the app installed.

From the app

  1. Open cofounder and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Account → Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

By email

If you can no longer get into the app or don't have it installed, write to soporte@getcofounder.app with the subject “Delete account”, from your account's email. If your account has no email, tell us the name and age on your profile. We reply within 30 days at most.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, your preferences and your swipe decisions.
  • Your matches and, with them, the messages in those conversations, including those the other person wrote.
  • Your blocks.
  • The GitHub link, if you had one.
  • Your profile photo and your board photos, with their captions: they stop being accessible when the account is deleted, and the server tries to remove them from storage. If a removal fails, an unlinked file may remain until we remove it. If you want confirmation of removal, write to us.

What is kept and for how long

  • Provider backups: up to 30 more days.
  • Providers' technical logs: 90 days at most.
  • Abuse reports in which you appear, as the reporter or the reported person: 12 months at most from their creation. They are kept even if you delete your account, with no link to your profile.

We may delete accounts that have gone 24 months without access. We do not send any automatic notice beforehand.

Signing out or uninstalling the app does not delete your data.
